Privacy and texting terms

These terms cover the HairBySusy On-Call Team sign-up at crew.hairbysusy.com and the job messages that follow. Last updated November 2026.

Who is responsible

HairBySusy is responsible for your information. Contact us at info@hairbysusy.com about anything on this page.

We never sell your information

HairBySusy does not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. Mobile numbers and texting consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email address, Instagram handle, an optional portfolio link, the locations where you can work, the kind of work you want, your specialties, how fast you can reply, your agreement to receive job texts, and your agreement to the On-Call Team rule.

Why we use it

Only to review your application and to contact you about jobs with HairBySusy. We rely on the consent you give when you apply. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it for us

A small number of service providers store or send your information on our behalf and may not use it for anything else: our website host (Netlify), the business system where we keep our contact list (Wix), our email provider (Google), the private workspace where we keep the crew list (Anthropic), and our texting provider (Sent). These services are based in the United States, so your information may be stored there. Inside HairBySusy, only the people who run the On-Call Team can see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you're on the team. If you're not approved, or you leave or are removed, we delete them within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Job texts

If you agree to receive texts, HairBySusy sends job availability texts to the number you gave. Each text names the location, date and time of a job and asks if you're available. Message frequency varies with how often jobs come up. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or email info@hairbysusy.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop hearing from us. Email info@hairbysusy.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the data protection authority where you live.

Money

HairBySusy never asks anyone to pay to join the team or to get a job. If someone does, it isn't us. Report it to info@hairbysusy.com.
