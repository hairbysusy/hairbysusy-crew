Privacy and texting terms

These terms cover the Hairbysusy On-Call Team sign-up at crew.hairbysusy.com and the job texts that follow. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email address, Instagram handle, an optional portfolio link, the locations where you can work, the kind of work you want, your specialties, how fast you can confirm, and your agreement to receive job texts.

How we use it

Only to review your application and to contact you about jobs with Hairbysusy. We do not sell your information. Mobile numbers and texting consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Applications are received and stored by our website host and kept in Hairbysusy's private crew list. We use a texting provider to send job texts.

Job texts

If you agree to receive texts, Hairbysusy sends job availability texts to the number you gave. Each text names the location, date and time of a job and asks if you're available. Message frequency varies with how often jobs come up. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or email info@hairbysusy.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Money

Hairbysusy never asks anyone to pay to join the team or to get a job. If someone does, it isn't us. Report it to info@hairbysusy.com.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, email info@hairbysusy.com and we'll take care of it.
