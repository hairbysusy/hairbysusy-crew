The On-Call Team rule

Before you apply

Susy's clients book on short notice. When you reply YES to a job, you are booked, and Susy has given her word to her client.

  • Replying YES is a commitment.
  • If you back out of a confirmed job within 24 hours of it, by cancelling, going silent or opting out of texts, you will be permanently removed from the Hairbysusy On-Call Team and will not be offered jobs again.
  • If you don't show up, you will be permanently removed.
  • You can stop job texts at any time by replying STOP. If you are not booked on a job, there is no penalty.
