How it works

  1. 01

    Post the job

    City, neighborhood, date, call time, the work and your budget. Your exact address comes after you're matched.

  2. 02

    Stylists say yes

    We send it to stylists who take clients in your city.

  3. 03

    We check, then match

    Our team reviews their work and calls them. Then we match you.

Booking fee

30% of your budget, paid when you post. Refunded in full if we can't match you. Non-refundable once you're matched. You pay your stylist directly.

Your details

Your stylist gets your first name, and the address the day before the job. Calls and texts go through HBS, so your number stays private.
